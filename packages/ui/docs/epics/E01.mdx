# E01 · Mentor page reached by share link (#8)

An invited mentor publishes a page under their own link within 20 minutes, sets availability, and a developer who opens the link sees who the mentor is, the prices and open slots — with no ratings (D03, D08, N02).

## Flow

| # | Step | Who | Screen | Spec |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A founder sends a personal invitation link (`npm run mentor:invite`) | Founder | — (CLI) | #14 |
| 2 | „Oliwia zaprasza cię do DevMentora” → „Akceptuję zaproszenie” | Mentor | S1 `/invite/[token]` | #14 |
| 3 | Five fields + link, „Opublikuj stronę” → share link with „Kopiuj link” | Mentor | S4 `/mentor` | #14 |
| 4 | Weekly windows in the mentor's zone, preview of the next 10 slots | Mentor | S5 `/mentor/availability` | #15 |
| 5 | Identity → public work → description → prices → slots | Developer | S6 `/m/[slug]` | #16 |
| 6 | Coming back on a new laptop: invitation-only message, founder sends a sign-in link | Mentor | S3, S2 | #14 |
