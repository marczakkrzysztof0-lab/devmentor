# Pattern: Time-zone-aware slots

Mentor and developer can be in different zones; a slot is stored in UTC and always shown in the zone of the person looking at it (#15, #16, #17).

## Rules

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| The developer sees slots in the browser's zone, and the zone is printed in the heading: „Najbliższe terminy (twoja strefa: Europe/Warsaw)” | Spec #16 |
| Unknown or invalid zone → UTC, labelled „(czas UTC)” | Spec #16 |
| The mentor edits and previews in the mentor's own zone; a window keeps its wall-clock time across DST („18:00” stays 18:00) | Spec #15 |
| The date is always in the text of a slot, not only in the group heading (screen readers get „jutro, wtorek 20.10, 18:00”) | Spec #16 |
| Choosing 50 min hides starts where 50 minutes do not fit | Spec #16 story 2 |
| Times in Geist Mono; relative days only for today and tomorrow („dziś”, „jutro”), otherwise „czw. 22.10” | DK2; proposal C14 (day format) |

## States

| State | Story |
|---|---|
| Viewer's zone | viewer's zone in the heading |
| Another zone | same slots seen from London |
| Zone unknown | zone unknown → UTC, labelled |
| DST change | DST change on 25.10 keeps the mentor's 18:00 |
| Lots of slots | lots of slots, by day |
