# Panel

## 1. Purpose
Sets one block of main content slightly apart from the page so the user finds it at once — for example the box with the link to save.

## 2. When to use
- „Zapisz ten link” on `/r/{token}` — the save-the-link box is main content (#17).
- The page editor form on `/mentor` (#14).
- The question block on the mentor's request detail (#18).

## 3. When not to use
| Situation | Use instead | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Separating rows of a list | `List` (1 px lines) | R01.1, R01.3 |
| Separating page sections | Space (`gap-between-sections`) and headings | Shape and depth, A01 |
| Inside another panel | Nothing — never a card in a card | audyt.md, A01 |
| A message about a state | `Alert` or `StateMessage` | — |

## 4. Anatomy
Surface (`surface-panel`, 1 px `border-divider`, 4 px, `p-inside-panel`) → optional title (section heading, makes it a labelled region) → content.

## 5. Variants
<Canvas of={Stories.Variants} />

| Variant | When |
|---|---|
| With `title` | The block has its own heading („Zapisz ten link”) — becomes a `<section>` region |
| Without title | Pure grouping, e.g. a line of context |

No shadow variant, no coloured variants. Source: P2, A01.

## 6. States
Panel is not interactive; hover, focus, pressed, disabled, loading, selected and error do not apply. Controls inside carry their own states.

## 7. Behaviour and accessibility
- With `title`: `<section aria-labelledby>` → a landmark region screen-reader users can jump to.
- `titleAs` keeps the heading order correct (h2 on a page, h3 inside a section). Source: WCAG 1.3.1.

## 8. Content
Title: noun phrase or instruction of 1–4 words („Zapisz ten link”). Body text keeps `max-w-text`.

## 9. Responsiveness
Full width of its column at every width; padding stays 16 px.

## 12. Do and don't
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| One panel for the save-the-link box on the request page. | Every section in its own card — the page becomes a stack of cards (A01). |
| A border of 1 px, no shadow. | `shadow-lg` floating cards (A01). |

## 13. Related
- Confused with: `Alert`, `List`.
- Used in: Request status page, Share link, Form pattern (editor).
