# D11 — When neither party posts anything in the session chat during the booked slot, it counts as the mentor's no-show: the mentee is refunded in full, automatically
- Date, owner: 2026-10-16, Krzysztof Marczak; source: Krzysztof Marczak, chat 2026-10-16; D05; backlog dry run `.ai/analysis/2026-10-16-backlog-dry-run.md` (E03-S02 blocked because R09 and R10 both apply when neither party posts)
- Context and the options weighed: D05 defines a no-show as a party who posts nothing in the session chat during the slot. When both are silent, R09 (full refund on the mentor's no-show) and R10 (the mentor keeps the payment on the mentee's no-show) contradict each other. (a) treat it as the mentor's no-show: full automatic refund; (b) treat it as the mentee's no-show: the mentor keeps the payment; (c) split the payment or review the case by hand.
- Decision and why: (a). The mentor accepted the slot and is the one providing the service; a refund the same day without a support ticket is what D05 already promises when a session does not happen through the mentor (2026-09-02 freelancer interview). (c) would need a dispute owner, who does not exist yet (Q05 in the brief). Tradeoff: a mentor who was present but wrote nothing is not paid; posting in the chat is what counts as showing up.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: the brief adds R15, which supersedes R10, and the glossary's no-show entry refers to it; E03-S02 is no longer blocked. Revisit after the pilot (D09) if silent sessions occur.
